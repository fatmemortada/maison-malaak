About Maison Malak

A space for transformation

I'm Malak, founder of Maison Malaak and an eye beauty specialist with more than eight years of experience. As a certified esthetician and permanent makeup artist, my passion is to enhance every woman's natural beauty through refined, precise, personalized treatments.

I created Maison Malaak as a space where elegance, calm and expertise come together. Every appointment is designed to feel peaceful and luxurious while highlighting your natural features.

My goal is simple: to help you feel beautiful, confident and radiant in a welcoming space where you feel heard and cared for.
